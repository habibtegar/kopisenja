A little about us

Good coffee,
good company.

Born from a love of late afternoon conversations, KOPI SENJA is a neighborhood coffee shop built around the simple joy of a really good cup.

We work closely with local farmers, roast our beans in small batches, and believe every detail matters: from the first pour to the last goodbye.
